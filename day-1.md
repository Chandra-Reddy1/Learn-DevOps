**What is EKS?**
EKS is AWS's managed Kubernetes service that reduces operational overhead by managing the Kubernetes control plane, while providing seamless integration with AWS services.

**2.Why EKS and not Kubernetes?**
**Kubernetes (Self-managed)**

You must install, maintain, patch, and upgrade the Kubernetes control plane.
More operational effort.

**EKS (Managed Kubernetes by AWS)**
AWS manages the control plane for you.
Easier to operate and maintain.
Integrates easily with AWS services (IAM, Load Balancer, EBS, CloudWatch).
